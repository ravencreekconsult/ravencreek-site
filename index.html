Common questions

Asked often, answered straight.

Do you implement the changes you recommend?

No, and that works in your favor. Implementation retainers are expensive and create lock-in. We give you clear, specific findings that your own team or contractors can carry out, and we stay available to help them troubleshoot.

Will you try to sell me software?

No. We take no referral or affiliate fees from the tools we recommend. If we suggest a platform, it is because it fits your business and your budget.

Is this only for tech companies?

No. Most of the businesses we help are ordinary local and regional companies: trades, shops, services. The value is in your systems, not in how technical you are.

What does it cost?

The intro consult is a flat $100 for 10 minutes, credited in full toward your audit. The initial audit is a flat $950, one time. Add-on audits for a specific area of the business are discounted from there. Monthly advisory is quoted after your audit and varies by client, and the audit fee is credited toward your first month. Because we advise rather than implement, the cost runs well below an implementation retainer.
